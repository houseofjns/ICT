Exam Preparation

Assessment roadmap

Build both theory knowledge and practical ICT skills.

Paper 1

Written examination

Revise key terms, compare technologies, explain benefits and drawbacks, interpret scenarios and support judgements with reasons.

  • Learn precise definitions.
  • Use examples in explanation questions.
  • For “evaluate”, discuss both sides and reach a justified conclusion.
Paper 2

Practical examination

Practise completing tasks accurately in suitable software and checking the final result against requirements.

  • File management and naming.
  • Word processing, graphics and presentations.
  • Web authoring, spreadsheets and databases.
  • Save files in the required location and format.
